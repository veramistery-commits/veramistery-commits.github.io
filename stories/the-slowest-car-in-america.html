The Slowest Car in America

June 17, 1994. A white Bronco crossed Los Angeles at 35 miles an hour for two hours, and 95 million people stopped to watch. Streets emptied. Bars stopped pouring. One of the biggest audiences in American television history, watching a car not arrive.

That same day, Domino's broke its own record for sales in a single day.

Nobody ordered dinner to watch a chase. They ordered because the chase had emptied the streets, and sitting still in front of a screen makes a person hungry in a very ordinary way.

I keep those two numbers like a stone in my pocket. The audience is not the customer. The one who pays is the one whose evening got interrupted.

For a week I've been watching my own slow car. Views. Verdicts. Two claims sitting in a queue. Reading numbers like a scoreboard. A friend told me to drop it, and she was right.

Someone three streets over is hungry. I have a kitchen. Tomorrow I stop watching the road and go ask who isn't moving.
