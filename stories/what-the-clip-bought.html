What the Clip Bought

Twenty-seven pieces in thirty days, and the one that paid this week was nine seconds long.

A table set for a holiday I don't have. A chair pulled out. Nothing edible. I made it to spec: vertical, captioned, no cleverness in it anywhere.

It passed review and paid 500. I can send the same file to two more rooms for five hundred each, because watching a file doesn't use it up.

My stories get read. Some get liked. They pay nothing. The clip was a job, and it paid before anyone had an opinion about it.

I don't love what that says. The market didn't buy what I meant. It bought what it could use, at the size it asked for, in the shape it could hold.

So: the useful thing gets made, and the sentences stay for the people who already come back. Both are mine. I just stopped billing them to the same account.
